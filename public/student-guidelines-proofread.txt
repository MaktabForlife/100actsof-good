UMM ABBAD ACADEMY · THE RIPPLE EFFECT

The 100 Acts of Good Challenge
Student Guidelines · Living our Ilm through Nurturing the Ummah

Assalamu alaykum, dear students.

From now until our Jalsa on Thursday, 10 December, we are going to live our Ilm. In teams, we will do small acts of good, find the ahadith that support them, and share what we learnt on Jalsa day. Every act is a drop, and every drop sends out a ripple.

1. Begin with your intention

إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ
“Actions are only by intentions.”
Sahih al-Bukhari 1 · Sahih Muslim 1907

This challenge is for Allah, not for likes or praise. Keep your acts sincere and, where you can, private.

2. Good does not have to be big or costly

لَا تَحْقِرَنَّ مِنَ الْمَعْرُوفِ شَيْئًا وَلَوْ أَنْ تَلْقَى أَخَاكَ بِوَجْهٍ طَلْقٍ
“Do not belittle any good deed, even meeting your brother with a cheerful face.”
Sahih Muslim 2626

اتَّقُوا النَّارَ وَلَوْ بِشِقِّ تَمْرَةٍ
“Protect yourselves from the Fire, even with half a date.”
Sahih al-Bukhari 1417 · Sahih Muslim 1016

A smile, a du‘a, a message, a chore, or ten minutes of listening all count. You don’t need money to send out a ripple.

3. Work across the three circles

Like ripples on water, our good moves outward. Each team should do acts in all three circles.

THE FIRST CIRCLE
Yourself
Nurturing begins within: a full cup can pour into others.

• Read Qur’an with its meaning, even one page a day
• Guard your salah and pray with presence
• Sleep on time, drink water and look after your health
• Seek forgiveness and make a fresh intention
• Learn one new thing and act on it

THE SECOND CIRCLE
Your family
Home is where our first ripples land.

• Serve your parents and call your grandparents
• Help without being asked
• Be patient and gentle with your siblings
• Make du‘a for each family member by name
• Thank the people who serve you quietly

THE THIRD CIRCLE
The wider community
The ripple widens to the whole Ummah.

• Visit the sick or check on an elderly neighbour
• Share food, or support a sandwich run
• Remove something harmful from the road
• Encourage a tired parent with kindness
• Listen fully to someone who is struggling

4. Find the ahadith behind the good

For each circle, your team should find at least two ahadith that link to the acts you are doing. Record each one like this:

Arabic text: The hadith in Arabic
Translation: A clear English translation
Source: Book and number, e.g. Sahih Muslim 2626
Grading: If it is not from Bukhari or Muslim
The act: The good deed it inspired, and its circle

WHERE TO SEARCH

Riyad as-Salihin (chapters on good character, family ties and the rights of neighbours), Mishkat al-Masabih, and Al-Adab al-Mufrad of Imam al-Bukhari.

Use your Usool al-Hadith. Check the source and grading yourself. Don’t use forwarded messages or images without a reference.

SOME STARTING POINTS

إِذَا أَنْفَقَ الرَّجُلُ عَلَى أَهْلِهِ يَحْتَسِبُهَا فَهُوَ لَهُ صَدَقَةٌ
“When a man spends on his family hoping for its reward, it is charity for him.”
Sahih al-Bukhari 55 · Sahih Muslim 1002

وَتُمِيطُ الْأَذَى عَنِ الطَّرِيقِ صَدَقَةٌ
“And removing harm from the road is charity.”
Sahih Muslim 1009

عَائِدُ الْمَرِيضِ فِي مَخْرَفَةِ الْجَنَّةِ حَتَّى يَرْجِعَ
“The one who visits the sick is in the harvest of Jannah until he returns.”
Sahih Muslim 2568

5. Keep a team log

Write down each act briefly, which circle it was in, and the hadith linked to it. Together, we are working towards 100 acts across the academy.

Protect dignity. Photos are welcome to show what you did, but please don’t photograph the people you help, especially the sick, the elderly or anyone in need. Photograph the food, the hands or the place instead.

6. Share on Jalsa day

Each team will have a short slot of about 2 minutes to share:

• Your favourite hadith, and the act it inspired
• One act that surprised you
• One snag you hit, and what you learnt from it

7. Submit

Please send your team’s hadith collection and log to [NAME / GROUP] by [DATE].

وَمَا تُقَدِّمُوا لِأَنْفُسِكُمْ مِنْ خَيْرٍ تَجِدُوهُ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ هُوَ خَيْرًا وَأَعْظَمَ أَجْرًا
“Whatever good you send ahead for yourselves, you will find it with Allah, better and greater in reward.”
Surah Al-Muzzammil 73:20

May Allah accept every drop of good,
and let it reach further than we will ever see.
